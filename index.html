      What I build for small businesses

      Every project starts with the work that eats your week. Then I pick the AI approach that fits it, not the other way around.

      AI workflows & automation

      Connect the apps you already use so leads, follow-ups, invoices and reports move on their own.

      e.g. new lead → AI qualifies it → CRM updated → follow-up sent

      AI agents & voice agents

      Agents that answer customers, book appointments and handle calls after hours, with clear hand-offs to you.

      e.g. a voice agent that books jobs while you're on site

      Ask-your-documents assistants

      RAG applications that answer questions from your contracts, SOPs and policies, with the source shown.

      e.g. "What's our cancellation clause for this client?"

      AI content creation

      Posts, reels, whiteboard videos and ad creative produced with AI and kept in your brand voice.

      e.g. a month of social posts drafted from one call

      Custom business avatars

      An AI presenter for explainer videos, product walk-throughs and ads, so you don't have to film everything yourself.

      e.g. a welcome video for every new client

      AI adoption training

      Hands-on sessions that get your team using AI tools safely and confidently in their daily work.

      e.g. a half-day workshop on AI for your front desk
